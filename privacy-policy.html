d) The length of time a cookie stays on your device depends on its type. We use two types of cookies on the Website:


  • Session cookies are temporary cookies which only exist during the time You use the Website (or more strictly, until You close the browser after using the Website). Session cookies help the Website remember what You chose on the previous page, avoiding the need to re-enter information.
  • Persistent cookies stay on your device after You have visited the Website. For example, when You visit the Website, a persistent cookie will be used to remember your preferences, so that the system remembers your choice the next time You visit the Website. Persistent cookies help us to identify You as a unique visitor, but do not contain any information that could be used to identify You to another person.
